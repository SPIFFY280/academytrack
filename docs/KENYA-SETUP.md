# Kenyan university and college configuration

The fictional campus is an example, not an approved curriculum or national grading standard.

## Configure before a real cohort

1. Open Institution & programmes as a department officer.
2. Set institution name, active period, registration deadline and report response target.
3. Create programmes with verified qualification names, KNQF levels, awarding/assessing bodies and credit conventions.
4. Choose university or technical college, semester/trimester/term and GPA, percentage or competency results.
5. Enter approved grade bands, pass thresholds and component weights. Weights total 100; thresholds include zero. Leave unused grade rows blank.
6. Set completion credits, project and attachment requirements. Credits are locally defined; the app does not convert them to KNQF notional hours.
7. Create units/modules, assign trainers and configure programme requirements, prerequisites and offered periods.
8. Assign each student's programme before results are recorded.
9. Verify finance, examination and clearance procedures with authorised officers.

GPA maps confirmed numeric marks to configured points, weighted by credits. Percentage mode shows a credit-weighted mean. Competency mode records competent, not yet competent or not assessed with feedback, without converting these outcomes into GPA.

Numeric components must all be confirmed before a final total is calculated. An incomplete component removes the previous numeric total. A manually entered final total clears its old breakdown so the records do not contradict each other.

Rules lock after results exist. Approved policy changes and transfers require a reviewed migration.

## Scope

Supported workflows include unit/module registration, CATs and exams, missing marks, departmental escalation, special/supplementary requests, industrial attachment/project clearance, KES fees and payment queries, and self-reported funding organisation.

Resit caps, retake replacement policies, attendance requirements, award classification, progression boards, RPL, credit transfer, external moderation and certification are not automatically inferred. Map those institution-specific rules before using this as a formal system of record. Department officers have institution-wide academic access within one installation.

## Official design references

- [TVETA standards](https://www.tveta.go.ke/tvet-standards/): CBETA, assessment and industrial attachment.
- [KNQA framework](https://knqa.go.ke/qualifications-framework/): academic, TVET and industry pathways.
- [KNQA qualifications database](https://nqd.knqa.go.ke/registered-qualifications/): verify award details and levels.
- [CUE programmes](https://www.cue.or.ke/index.php?Itemid=190&catid=13&id=22&option=com_content&view=article): university programme reference.
- [KU service responsibilities](https://www.ku.ac.ke/guidelines_for_provision_of_services_to_students/): registration, marks, exams and finance workflows.
- [HEF FAQs](https://www.hef.co.ke/faqs/): official funding tracking and appeals.

These are references, not integrations, accreditation or endorsements. The institution's currently approved curriculum and handbook take precedence over examples.
